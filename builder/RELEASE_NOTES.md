## Patch notes: 1.2.1

Fixes for 1.2:

- **Pushing crates works again.** In 1.2, crates and other pushable objects couldn't be moved at all.
- **Breakable crates can be pushed.** Crates that can also be broken (most of those in Office Complex) could never be pushed, even before 1.2.
- **Pulling.** Hold Use while looking at a crate and walk backwards to drag it along, as on PC.
- **The elevator in We've Got Hostiles** (after the first soldier) no longer vanishes at the loading screen halfway up and drops you back into the previous area. It carries on to the top in the next map.
- **Things that carry over between maps** (lifts, trains, doors, breakables) now arrive where they were. Before, they could arrive hundreds of units away.

For everything new in 1.2, see [What's new](https://github.com/ToxyCola/Half-Life-Nintendo-64-Port#whats-new-in-12) in the README.
